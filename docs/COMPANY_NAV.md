# Company section — where to click

## Full Company hub (hero, metrics, all tabs)

1. Start `npm run dev`
2. Open http://localhost:3000
3. In the **top Header** navigation, click **Company** (building icon)
4. You should see:
   - Hero (Melbourne HQ)
   - Metrics bar
   - Tabs: **About | Contact | Newsletters | Podcast | Trust Centre | Legal**

## Footer (bottom of page)

The footer only has **shortcuts** into Company:

- About & Company
- Contact
- Newsletters
- Legal
- Advertise / Media kit

Those open the same **Company** pillar (header item).
